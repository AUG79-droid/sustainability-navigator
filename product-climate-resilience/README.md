# Product Climate Resilience Challenge — curriculum v2

Eight bilingual modules, 72 theory sections, 64 formative decisions and a mandatory integrated fictional 2050 review dossier. The dossier has seven participant-written evidence fields, a review reference after submission, and TXT export. Automated completion checks do not grade technical correctness. No real aircraft performance, authorised limits, company data, operational procedure or approved engineering design. No internal article is published. All exercise numbers are fictitious. The Navigator's existing four-question paired formative probe remains a narrow learning sample; it does not establish mastery or causality.
